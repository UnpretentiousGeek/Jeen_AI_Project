# KYB Onboarding Policy — Version 1.0

## KYB-1.1 Entity identity

The legal name, registration number, jurisdiction, and registered address must be supported by current incorporation evidence. Material conflicts must be resolved before the case is marked ready for review.

## KYB-1.2 Beneficial ownership

The applicant must identify every natural person who owns or controls 25% or more of the entity. The submitted ownership chain must account for 100% of ownership or explain the remainder with supporting evidence.
